How Sahyog Sutra Works

1. Create a Campaign

Submit a community campaign across 10 categories and 100+ event types — from environment drives to hackathons. Add your dates, location, and description — or let AI write it for you. Your event goes live after admin approval.

2. Explore & Engage

Browse campaigns by category, search by keyword, and filter by what matters to you. Like events to show support, share them on WhatsApp, add them to your calendar, and translate them into your preferred language.

3. Connect & Collaborate

Every campaign has a built-in group chat. Coordinate with organizers and other participants in real time. View organizer profiles, track top contributors on the leaderboard, and be part of the community.
